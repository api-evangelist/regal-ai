Enterprises need voice agents that hold a natural, human-like conversation while moving each call toward its goal and staying within compliance boundaries. The Voice CX Benchmark scores models on exactly that: how well they progress the call turn by turn, how safely and clearly they communicate, and how quickly they respond.
